🍺Nightlife: Bars & Clubs

Best Bars: Atmosphere & Craft Vibes

For those who want conversation, quality drinks, and distinct vibes.

Sheffield Pub (Kaleiçi/Old Town)

The Vibe: A slice of London in the Mediterranean. Sheffield Pub is widely regarded as the best spot for craft beer lovers. It avoids the "tourist trap" feel, offering a cozy, wood-paneled interior and a relaxed garden area.

Best For: Craft beer flights, casual evenings, and escaping the thumping bass of nearby clubs.

Drink Pick: Ask for their Turkish craft beer selection (Gara Guzu is a local favorite).

The Vibe: Sophisticated, upscale, and acoustic. Located in the Akra Hotel, this is a world-class jazz bar. The lighting is dim and moody, the furniture is plush velvet, and the acoustics are engineered for live performances.

Best For: Jazz enthusiasts, date nights, and "grown-up" cocktails.

Music: Live Jazz, Blues, Soul, and Funk.

Up Shot Bar (Kaleiçi/Old Town)

The Vibe: High energy on a narrow street. Up Shot is the heart of the "Street Party" scene in Old Town. The DJ plays to the crowd standing in the alleyway. It is gritty, authentic, and incredibly fun.

Best For: Meeting locals and dancing without the commitment of a massive nightclub.

Music: Electronic, Deep House, and DJ sets.

Nightclubs: The "Big Three"

Antalya’s clubbing scene is split. For massive open-air spectacles, you go to Kemer (45 mins from center). For chic, dressy clubs, you stay in Lara.

Ceila Club (Lara/City Center)

The Vibe: "See and be seen." Ceila is an open-air venue right on the cliffs. It is stylish and attracts a well-dressed crowd of locals and expats. The decor is modern with neon accents and VIP lounges.

Music: Commercial House, Turkish Pop remixes, and Top 40.

Note: Dress code is strictly enforced here. Avoid beachwear.

Club Inferno & Aura Club (Kemer)

The Vibe: Absolute chaos (in a good way). These are the "Super-Clubs" of the Turkish Riviera. Located in Kemer, they hold thousands of people. Expect foam parties, go-go dancers, fire shows, and celebrity guest DJs.

Music: EDM (Electronic Dance Music), Big Room House, and Hardstyle.

Logistics: These are 45+ minutes from Antalya center. Most hotels run shuttle buses; otherwise, a private transfer is required.

The Vibe: A rising star in the Lara district. It sits on the cliffs with a fantastic view and offers a hybrid experience—restaurant by evening, pumping club by night.

Music: Mainstream hits and performance DJ sets.

Rooftop Bars: Best Views

For the perfect Instagram shot or a romantic sunset.

Asmani Restaurant & Bar (Lara)

The View: Unbeatable. Located on the rooftop of the Akra Hotel, it offers a 360-degree panoramic view of the Bey Dağları mountains and the Mediterranean Sea.

The Experience: Fine dining meets high-end mixology. It is pricey but worth it for the sunset hour.

Tipsy Old Town (Kaleiçi)

The View: A terrace overlooking the iconic terracotta rooftops of the Old Town with the sea in the background.

The Experience: bohemian and laid-back. It feels like a secret garden above the city. Great for wine and chilled electronic beats.

Castle Café & Bar (Kaleiçi)

The View: Located right on the edge of the cliffs near Hıdırlık Tower. You are practically dangling over the harbor.

The Experience: Tourist-friendly and casual. Go here for a late-afternoon beer to watch the boats return to the marina.
