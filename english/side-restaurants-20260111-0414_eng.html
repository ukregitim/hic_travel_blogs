Side

🍽️Best Restaurants

Karma Restaurant & Night Club

The Vibe: This is arguably the most famous spot in Side’s Old Town. By day and early evening, it’s a chic restaurant with a stunning terrace overlooking the Mediterranean. By night, it transforms into a sophisticated club. What to Order: The Chateaubriand is legendary here—tender, flavorful, and carved tableside. If you're into seafood, their sushi platters are surprisingly good for a non-Japanese specialist. Insider Tip: Book a table on the edge of the terrace about 45 minutes before sunset. The view of the sun dipping into the sea is unmatched.

Location: Old Town, near the Harbor.

Azumare Lounge

The Vibe: Laid-back luxury. Azumare is less about white tablecloths and more about sipping wine on comfortable cushions while waves crash against the rocks just feet away. It feels like a hidden corner of paradise. What to Order: They do an excellent "Kokoreç" in a croissant (a modern twist on a Turkish classic). Their burger selection is also top-tier if you need a break from kebabs. Insider Tip: It requires a bit of a walk to the end of the peninsula, but the seclusion is part of the charm.

Location: Harbor end.

🔥 Authentic Turkish: The Real Deal

Best for: Traditionalists, meat lovers, and "Ocakbaşı" culture.

Kamer Ocakbaşı Restaurant

The Vibe: "Ocakbaşı" refers to a restaurant where customers can sit near the grill and watch the chef work. Kamer offers this authentic experience with a view of the sea, blending rustic culinary traditions with a lovely atmosphere. What to Order: You are here for the Lamb Chops (Pirzola) or the Adana Kebab. The meat is charcoal-grilled to perfection—smoky, juicy, and spicy. Don't skip the meze platter to start; their Haydari (yogurt dip) is exceptionally creamy.

Location: Side Old Town.

Price: $$ (Moderate)

Gül Restaurant

The Vibe: A family-run gem that feels like you’ve been invited into a Turkish home. It’s away from the most aggressive tourist traps and offers a warm, hospitable environment. What to Order: The Testi Kebab (Pottery Kebab). This is a theatrical dish where meat and vegetables are slow-cooked in a sealed clay pot, which is then broken open at your table with a small hammer. It’s tender, stew-like, and delicious.

Location: Side Town Center.

🐟 Seafood & Fine Dining

Best for: Special occasions and fresh Mediterranean catches.

Side Orfoz Restaurant

The Vibe: Elegant and refined. Orfoz is widely regarded as one of the best seafood restaurants in the region. It’s quiet, professional, and focuses heavily on the quality of ingredients. What to Order: Ask for the Catch of the Day (usually Sea Bass or Sea Bream) grilled whole. If it's in season, the Grilled Octopus is tender and not at all rubbery.

Location: Near the Harbor.

Q Beach Restaurant & Lounge

The Vibe: Modern, airy, and stylish. Located in the Evrenseki area (a short drive from Old Town), this is where you go for a "beach club" lunch that turns into a dinner. What to Order: They excel at Calamari and fresh prawns. They also offer a robust menu of international dishes and have solid Vegan/Vegetarian options (like gluten-free pasta and fresh salads), which can be harder to find in traditional kebab houses.

Location: Evrenseki Beach.

🥩 Casual Favorites & Hidden Gems

Best for: A relaxed meal, families, and comfort food.

Old Town Steakhouse

The Vibe: Tucked away in the winding streets of the historic center, this place is often missed by day-trippers. It’s cozy, rustic, and serious about its meat. What to Order: As the name suggests, go for the Ribeye Steak. They also have a surprisingly extensive wine list featuring local Turkish wines (try a glass of Öküzgözü red wine).

Location: Old Town side streets.

Side House Café & Bar

The Vibe: Friendly, bustling, and versatile. It’s a great all-rounder located on the main Liman street. What to Order: Great for breakfast or a casual lunch. Their Turkish Breakfast spread is generous, and they serve excellent coffee. It's a safe bet for families with picky eaters as they serve everything from pasta to traditional clay pots.

Location: Liman Street.

📝 Practical Foodie Tips for Side

Reservations: For Karma and Orfoz, reservations are essential during the high season (June–September).

The "Cover" Charge: Some restaurants may add a "kuver" (cover charge) to the bill. This is standard practice in Turkey and usually covers the bread and water served immediately.

Tipping: A tip of 10–15% is customary and appreciated. Cash is preferred for tips even if you pay the bill by card.

Cats: You will see cats everywhere, including in restaurants. It is a cultural norm in Turkey to treat them kindly; they are generally clean and well-fed.
