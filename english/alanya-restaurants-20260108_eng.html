Alanya

🍽️Best Restaurants

Authentic Local & Traditional

To experience the true soul of Alanya, you must move away from the tourist-heavy beachfronts and seek out these long-standing favorites.

Ravza Restaurant: A local institution since 1955. Located near the bazaar, it is famous for its İskender kebab and traditional Turkish starters. It’s alcohol-free and focuses entirely on the quality of the meat and fresh-baked bread.

Yöresel Alanya Ev Mutfağı (Esma Hanım’ın Yeri): If you want to try "Alanya Home Cooking," this is the place. It is one of the few spots serving regional specialties like Güllük Çorbası (a hearty soup with chickpeas and meatballs) and Öksüz Helvası (a dark, molasses-based dessert).

Flash Restaurant: Don't let the modern decor fool you; this is one of the best places for Turkish Breakfast (Kahvaltı) and late-night soups. Their lentil soup and "Pide" are local staples.

Fine Dining & Panoramic Views

Alanya is famous for its dramatic cliffs. These restaurants offer high-end service paired with the best views in the city.

Kale Panorama Restoran: Perched high on the road to Alanya Castle, this restaurant offers a literal bird’s-eye view of the harbor. It’s the top choice for a romantic sunset dinner. The menu features a mix of Mediterranean seafood and premium steaks.

Seasons Restaurant (by Mutlu Şevket Yılmaz): Located in the Oba district, this is widely considered Alanya’s premier fine-dining destination. It offers a fusion of French and Mediterranean techniques, emphasizing seasonal, locally sourced ingredients.

Dinner Restaurant: Known for its sophisticated atmosphere and meticulous plating. It’s a "special occasion" spot that specializes in international gourmet cuisine and has an impressive wine list.

Fresh Seafood By The Harbor

As a coastal city, Alanya's seafood is exceptional, particularly in the historic harbor (İskele) area.

Mahperi Restaurant: One of the oldest and most respected waterfront establishments. It offers an elegant dining experience right on the water. Their Salt-Crusted Sea Bass is a signature dish that is as theatrical as it is delicious.

Harem Alanya: A more modern, upscale take on the traditional fish house. They are known for gourmet seafood platters, lobster, and shrimp cocktails, served in a very chic, "lounge" style environment.

Sultan Sofrası: A great balance of traditional Turkish hospitality and fresh Mediterranean catch. It’s popular for its wide selection of "Mezze" (small appetizers) that vary daily based on what’s fresh.

Trendy Grills & Atmosphere

If you want a lively vibe, high-quality cocktails, and perfectly grilled meats, these are the current "it" spots.

Mezze Grill Restaurant & Ocakbaşı: Consistently rated as one of the best in the city. The "Ocakbaşı" style means you can watch the chefs grill your meat over open coals. Their Mixed Grill Platter is legendary.

Soul of Kitchen: A stylish, Mediterranean-focused spot that attracts a younger, trendy crowd. The decor is Instagram-ready, and the menu leans toward fresh salads, seafood, and modern pasta dishes.

Lost in Alanya: A gastropub/restaurant hybrid with an extensive wine selection and a very cool, "hidden garden" vibe. Great for those who want a break from traditional kebabs.

Pro Tips for Dining in Alanya:

Reservations: For "Kale Panorama" or "Seasons," always book 24-48 hours in advance, especially during the summer months.

Tipping: A tip of 10-15% is standard in mid-to-high-end restaurants if the service isn't already included.

The "Mezze" Trap: In many traditional restaurants, waiters will bring a tray of cold appetizers (Mezze) to your table. You only pay for what you take, but it’s easy to fill up before your main course arrives!
