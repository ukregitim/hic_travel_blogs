Manavgat

🍽️Best Restaurants

Orfoz Restaurant (Side Old Town)

The Best for: Fine Dining & Sunsets Widely considered the jewel of the region's dining scene, Orfoz offers an unrivaled location right next to the Temple of Apollo. This is where you go for a romantic evening. The seafood is incredibly fresh, and the culinary technique is a step above standard tourist fare.

Cuisine: Seafood / Mediterranean

Signature Dish: Grilled Octopus or the Sea Bass in Salt Crust.

Vibe: Elegant, romantic, and serene with the sound of waves crashing nearby.

Kamer Ocakbaşı Restaurant

The Best for: Authentic Turkish Grills If you want to experience the true culture of an Ocakbaşı (where meat is grilled over charcoal right in front of you), this is the spot. Located in Side but easily accessible from Manavgat, it combines high-quality meats with exceptional hospitality.

Cuisine: Turkish Steakhouse / BBQ

Signature Dish: Adana Kebab or the Lamb Chops (Kuzu Pirzola).

Vibe: Warm, inviting, and aromatic.

My Corner Restaurant

The Best for: A Hidden Local Gem Away from the flashy tourist traps, this family-run establishment feels like eating at a Turkish friend's home. The service is personal, and the food is cooked with genuine love. It is famous for its theatrical clay pot dishes.

Cuisine: Traditional Turkish / Ottoman

Price: $-$$ (Affordable)

Signature Dish: Testi Kebab (Pottery Kebab)—broken open at your table with a hammer.

Vibe: Cozy, intimate, and incredibly friendly.

Q Beach Restaurant & Lounge

The Best for: Beachfront Dining Located on the Evrenseki strip, Q Beach is perfect for a relaxed lunch that turns into dinner. The atmosphere is chic but laid-back, offering high-quality international dishes alongside Turkish favorites right on the sand.

Cuisine: International / Seafood / Turkish

Signature Dish: Garlic Prawns and their Cocktail selection.

Vibe: Chill, stylish, and breezy.

Kalamar Balık Evi (Manavgat Center)

The Best for: Local Seafood While Side has the views, Manavgat center has the authenticity. Kalamar Balık Evi is where the locals go for fish. It’s unpretentious, reasonably priced, and focuses entirely on the freshness of the catch.

Signature Dish: Fried Calamari (Kalamar Tava) with their house-made tartar sauce.

Vibe: Casual, bustling, and authentic.

Old Town Steakhouse

The Best for: Carnivores Sometimes you just crave a high-quality steak. Old Town Steakhouse stands out for its dry-aged cuts and professional service. It provides a nice break from the traditional kebab scene if you are looking for a European-style steak dinner.

Signature Dish: Pepper Steak or the T-Bone.

Vibe: Sophisticated and rustic.

Allaturca Restaurant

The Best for: Families & Groups Allaturca manages to balance tourist appeal with genuine quality. It is a crowd-pleaser with a massive menu that executes everything well, from Italian pastas to Turkish casseroles.

Cuisine: International / Turkish

Signature Dish: Osmanlı Tabağı (Ottoman Plate)—a sampler of various stews and meats.

Vibe: Lively, spacious, and family-friendly.

Side Meyhane

The Best for: The "Meze" Experience A Meyhane is a traditional tavern where the focus is on small plates (meze), Raki (anise spirit), and conversation. This spot offers a fantastic array of cold appetizers that can easily make up a whole meal.

Cuisine: Turkish Meze / Tavern

Signature Dish: Grilled Octopus Salad and Fava Bean Puree.

Vibe: Traditional, musical, and social.

Almina Restaurant (Gündoğdu)

The Best for: Budget-Friendly Flavor Located in the Gündoğdu area, this is a top-rated spot for those who want high-quality food without the "sea-view tax." The kebabs are juicy, and the portions are generous.

Cuisine: Turkish / Mediterranean

Signature Dish: Chicken Shish and their fresh Lavash bread.

Vibe: Simple, clean, and focused on food.

Liman Restaurant

The Best for: Riverside Atmosphere You cannot visit Manavgat without dining by the famous river. While many riverside spots are touristy, Liman consistently offers good service and a reliable menu, making it the best pick for that iconic "eating by the green water" experience.

Cuisine: Turkish / Seafood

Signature Dish: Fresh River Trout (Alabalık) baked with mushrooms and cheese.

Vibe: Scenic and relaxing.

💡 Pro-Tips for Dining in Manavgat

Reservations: For sunset spots like Orfoz or Kamer, book at least 2 days in advance during high season (June-August).

Transport: Taxis are readily available, but if you are staying in a resort, ask if the restaurant offers a shuttle service—many of the better establishments (like steak houses) will pick you up for free!

The "Cover" Charge: Be aware that some restaurants add a "kuver" (cover charge) per person, which includes bread and water. This is standard practice in Turkey.
