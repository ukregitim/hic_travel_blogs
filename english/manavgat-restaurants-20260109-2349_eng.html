Manavgat

🍽️Best Restaurants

Meşhur 49 Pide Kebap Salonu (Manavgat Town) The Local’S Favorite This Is Widely Considered The Best Price-To-Performance Spot In Town. It Is Bustling, Loud, And Incredibly Authentic. It Is Not A Tourist Trap; It Is Where The Locals Go For A Feast.

Must Order: Pide (Turkish pizza)—specifically the Kuşbaşılı Pide (diced meat). Also, try the Tavuk Çoban Kavurma (Chicken Shepherd’s Roast), which comes bubbling in a clay pot.

Insider Tip: Portions are huge. You will likely receive complimentary appetizers (mezze), so don't over-order.

Kamer Ocakbaşı (Side) The Classic Grill Experience "Ocakbaşı" Refers To A Restaurant With A Large Open Charcoal Grill. At Kamer, You Can Watch The Chefs Skewer And Grill Meats Right In Front Of You. It Balances A Tourist-Friendly Location With Genuine Food Quality.

Must Order: Lamb Chops (Kuzu Pirzola) or the Mixed Grill. The meat here is famously tender.

Vibe: Warm and inviting, especially if you get a table near the grill pit to watch the action.

Sultan Sofrası (Manavgat River) Traditional Riverside Dining Located Directly On The Riverbank In The Town Center (Not The Waterfall Area), This Spot Offers A Serene Atmosphere Without The High Tourist Markups Of The Coast.

Must Order: Sultan Kebab (meat wrapped in pastry/crepe with cheese and sauce) or the Ali Nazik (meat over smoked eggplant puree).

II. Best Seafood & Waterfront Views

Best for: Romantic sunsets, fresh catch, and special occasions.

Orfoz Restaurant (Side Harbour) The Sunset Spot Located Near The Temple Of Apollo, This Is Arguably The Best Fine-Dining Seafood Spot In The Region. It Offers An Unobstructed View Of The Sunset Over The Mediterranean.

Must Order: Grilled Octopus (often cited as the best in town) and Lagor (Grouper) if available. Pair it with a bottle of Rakı.

Insider Tip: You must reserve a table 1–2 days in advance if you want a front-row seat for the sunset.

Taş Konak Restaurant (Manavgat River) River & Nature Situated Further Up The River Near The Waterfall District, This Restaurant Is Housed In A Beautiful Stone Mansion With A Garden Right On The Water’S Edge. It Is Quieter And Greener Than The Harbor Spots.

Must Order: Fresh Trout (Alabalık) or Sea Bream (Çipura) grilled on charcoal.

Vibe: Peaceful and romantic; you can hear the river flowing as you eat.

Q Beach Restaurant & Lounge (Evrenseki/Side) Modern & Chic If You Want A "Bodrum" Or "Ibiza" Vibe Rather Than Rustic Turkish, This Is The Place. It Features White Tablecloths, Sand Under Your Feet, And Stylish Cocktails.

Must Order: Calamari, Shrimp Casserole, and their signature cocktails.

III. Best Breakfast (Serpme Kahvaltı)

Turkish breakfast is a ritual, not a quick meal. Expect 20+ small plates of cheeses, jams, olives, and breads.

Bükmecim Kahvaltı A Hidden Gem For Breakfast Lovers. It Is Less About The View And More About The Food Quality—Homemade Jams, Fresh "Pişi" (Fried Dough), And Excellent Gözleme (Stuffed Flatbreads).

IV. Quick Summary & "Best For..."

Category Restaurant Why Go?

Best Budget/Local Meşhur 49 Huge portions, authentic taste, cheap prices.

Best Romantic View Orfoz (Side) Unbeatable sunset views by the ancient ruins.

Best Meat/Kebab Kamer Ocakbaşı High-quality charcoal grilled meats.

Best River Dining Taş Konak Peaceful nature setting on the Manavgat River.

Best Night Out Karma Fine dining that turns into a sophisticated club later.

Practical Tips for 2026

The "Waterfall" Trap: There are many restaurants immediately next to the Manavgat Waterfall entrance. While the view is nice, the food is often average and overpriced. It is better to visit the waterfall for a photo, then drive 5 minutes to Taş Konak or Sultan Sofrası for the actual meal.

Reservations: Essential for dinner in Side (Old Town) during high season (May–October).

Transport: Taxis are expensive. If you are staying in a hotel zone (Kumköy, Sorgun), the "Dolmuş" (shared minibus) is a cheap and reliable way to get to both Manavgat Town and Side Old Town.
