Authentic Turkish & Kebabs

Best for: Traditional flavors, charcoal grills, and "real" local food.

Mezze Grill Ocakbaşı

The Vibe: This is consistently rated as one of the top restaurants in Alanya for a reason. It balances a tourist-friendly atmosphere with deeply authentic cooking methods (Ocakbaşı means "fireside," referring to the open grill).

On the Menu: You are here for the meat. The Adana Kebab (spicy minced lamb) and Lamb Chops are charcoal-grilled to perfection.

📝 Professional Dining Tips for Alanya

The "Kuver" Charge: Many restaurants in Turkey add a small cover charge (kuver) to the bill for the bread and water served immediately. This is standard practice.

Tipping: Service is usually not included. A tip of 10-15% in cash is customary and highly appreciated by waitstaff.

Alcohol: While Alanya is very liberal, "Ocakbaşı" restaurants (traditional grills) sometimes do not serve alcohol, though most in tourist areas do. Always check the menu outside if a glass of Rakı is essential to your meal.

Reservations: For Terrace and Mahperi during July and August, reservations are mandatory if you want a table with a view.
