Manavgat

🍽️Best Restaurants

Best For Fine Dining & Ambiance (Side)

These locations are perfect for a romantic evening or a special celebration. They typically offer sunset views over the ruins or the sea.

Side Orfoz Restaurant

Vibe: Sophisticated and romantic. Located near the harbor with a deck right by the sea.

Cuisine: Seafood & International. Famous for fresh fish, calamari, and high-end service.

Best For: A sunset dinner where you can hear the waves crashing while sipping Turkish wine.

Karma Restaurant & Night Club

Vibe: Trendy and energetic. It starts as a high-quality restaurant and transitions into a stylish lounge/club later in the night.

Cuisine: International fusion. Steaks, sushi, and cocktails.

Best For: Couples or groups who want dinner followed by dancing without changing venues.

Serenade Restaurant & Bar

Vibe: Highly rated for its warm hospitality (often winning TripAdvisor awards). It has a Mexican-Steakhouse fusion concept.

Cuisine: Steaks, Fajitas, and Mexican dishes.

Best For: Visitors craving a break from Turkish food or wanting a perfectly cooked steak.

Best For Authentic Turkish Cuisine (Manavgat Center)

If you want to eat where the locals eat (and pay local prices), head into Manavgat town, away from the Side coast.

Cihan Pide Kebap Restaurant

Vibe: Casual, bustling, and unpretentious. This is a local institution.

Cuisine: Traditional Turkish Pide (flatbread pizza) and Kebabs.

Must Try: Kuşbaşılı Pide (diced meat pide) or the Mixed Grill.

Vibe: "Kasap" means butcher. This is a butcher-shop-turned-restaurant concept where you choose your meat from the counter, and they grill it for you.

Cuisine: Pure, high-quality meat. Lamb chops, meatballs (köfte), and sucuk.

Best For: Carnivores looking for the freshest meat in town.

Kamer Ocakbaşı (Side)

Vibe: Traditional "Ocakbaşı" style, where you can sit near the large copper grill and watch the chef work.

Cuisine: Adana Kebab, Lamb Shish, and Meze platters.

Best For: A traditional Turkish grill experience with a slightly more refined atmosphere than the town center spots.

Best For River Dining & Nature (Manavgat River/Falls)

Manavgat is famous for its river and waterfall. Dining here is about the cool breeze and the sound of flowing water.

Taş Konak Restaurant

Location: Near the Manavgat Waterfall (Şelale district).

Vibe: Rustic and peaceful. You are surrounded by trees and the river.

Cuisine: Traditional Turkish breakfast (Serpme Kahvaltı) and grilled trout.

Best For: A long, lazy brunch or a refreshing lunch after visiting the waterfalls.

Location: Further out, near the Oymapınar Dam (Green Canyon area).

Vibe: Breathtaking. Perched high up with views over the Green Lake.

Cuisine: Grilled fish (Trout), casseroles, and village-style food.

Best For: A "Hidden Gem" lunch escape. It requires a car or taxi, but the view is unmatched.

Best For Casual Beachside Eats

Q Beach Restaurant & Lounge

Location: Side Beach.

Vibe: Chic beach club atmosphere. Relaxed luxury with comfortable seating on the sand or deck.

Cuisine: Mediterranean, burgers, fresh salads, and cocktails.

Best For: A long lunch that turns into afternoon cocktails.

Practical Tips for Manavgat Dining

Reservations: For sunset spots in Side (like Orfoz or Karma), reservations are essential during the summer season (June–September).

Alcohol: Most restaurants in Side serve alcohol. In Manavgat Center, some traditional "Pide" spots may not serve alcohol, so check beforehand if you want a beer with your kebab.

Transport: Side is pedestrian-only in the old town. You will need to take a taxi or "Dolmuş" (minibus) to the entrance and walk to the harbor restaurants.
