Alanya

🍽️Best Restaurants

The Heavy Hitters: Authentic Turkish & Grills

If you want the quintessential Turkish "Ocakbaşı" (fireside grill) experience, these are the top-rated spots in the city center.

Mezze Grill Restaurant & Ocakbasi: Widely considered the best in town for its charcoal-grilled meats. Their signature mixed grill and freshly baked pita bread are legendary. It’s perfect for those who want high-quality Turkish food in a lively, central atmosphere.

Ravza Restaurant: A family-owned institution since 1955. Located near the harbor, it focuses on traditional dishes like Iskender kebab, Pide, and authentic mezes. It is a dry restaurant (no alcohol), making it a favorite for families and those seeking a purely traditional experience.

Flash Restaurant: Open late and popular with locals, Flash is famous for its hearty soups (Mercimek or Paça) and traditional Turkish breakfast. It’s the place to go if you want no-frills, high-flavor food that won't break the bank.

Dining With A View: Castle & Harbor

Alanya's geography—a massive peninsula with a castle on top—creates some of the most dramatic dining backdrops in the Mediterranean.

Kale Panorama Restaurant: Perched high on the road to Alanya Castle, this spot offers breathtaking views of the city lights and Cleopatra Beach. They even offer a free pick-up service from many hotels. It’s a top choice for romantic dinners or special celebrations.

Mahperi Restaurant: Situated right on the harbor since 1947, Mahperi offers a refined atmosphere where you can watch the pirate ships dock. They specialize in both steak and Mediterranean seafood, served with a classic touch.

Iskele Sofrası: Another harbor gem known as the "Father and Son Place," it’s famous for its hospitality and fresh fish. The view of the Red Tower at night is unbeatable.

Fine Dining & International Cuisine

For those seeking a more modern or "gourmet" twist on Mediterranean flavors.

Seasons Restaurant (by Mutlu Şevket Yılmaz): Known for its exceptional service and "chef’s touch." The menu changes with the seasons, focusing on fresh, local ingredients presented with artistic flair.

Olivia Gourmet: Located near the waterfront, this is a high-end choice for international cuisine. If you are craving a perfectly cooked steak or a sophisticated pasta dish paired with premium Turkish wines, this is the place.

Le Chevy Restaurant: A cozy but stylish spot known for Mediterranean fusion. Their lamb shank and grilled sea bream are frequently cited as the best in the Damlataş area.

Hidden Gems & Unique Experiences

Sometimes the best meals are found away from the main tourist strips.

Dim River (Dim Çayı) Restaurants: About 15 minutes from the city center, you can dine on wooden platforms floating directly over the cold mountain water of the Dim River. Most places here, like Pınarbaşı, serve fresh trout (Alabalık) caught right from the river.

Derya Lokantası: A true "tradesman’s restaurant" (Esnaf Lokantası). There is no fixed menu; you walk up to the counter and point at the daily home-cooked stews and vegetable dishes. It is incredibly cheap, healthy, and authentic.

Cafe Local: If you need a break from kebabs, this trendy cafe offers the best Neapolitan-style pizza and artisanal croissants in Alanya. It’s the "it" spot for brunch in 2026.
