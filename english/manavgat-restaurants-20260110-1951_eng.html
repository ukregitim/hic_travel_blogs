Top 10 Restaurants In Manavgat

These ten establishments consistently rank highest for food quality, service, and atmosphere.

Panorama Lake Restaurant

Cuisine: Turkish / Seafood Price: $$ Signature Dish: Oven-baked Trout (Alabalık) or Shepherd’s Roast (Çoban Kavurma) Why it’s Top 10: Located away from the city buzz at the Green Canyon lake, this spot offers a tranquil escape. You can swim in the lake while waiting for your food. It is rustic, authentic, and undeniably beautiful. Reservation: Recommended for weekends.

Karma Restaurant & Night Club

Cuisine: International / Fusion Price: $$$$ Signature Dish: Pepper Steak and their artisanal cocktails. Why it’s Top 10: A staple of Side’s nightlife and dining scene. It sits right on the water with a chic, modern vibe. The transition from a sunset dinner to a sophisticated night lounge is seamless. Reservation: Essential for dinner.

Dedem Kasap Cızbız

Cuisine: Turkish Steakhouse / Butcher (Kasap) Price: $$ Signature Dish: Grilled Meatballs (Köfte) and Lamb Chops (Kuzu Pirzola). Why it’s Top 10: A true local favorite in the Manavgat center. It operates as a butcher shop where you choose your meat cut at the counter, and they grill it to perfection. No frills, just incredible meat. Reservation: Not usually needed, but expect a wait at peak times.

Elia Restaurant & Lounge

Cuisine: Mediterranean / Fine Dining Price: $$$ Signature Dish: Grilled Octopus and Seafood Risotto. Why it’s Top 10: Known for its "boho-chic" design and stunning views of the Side harbor. The plating is artistic, and the ambiance is sophisticated yet relaxed. Reservation: Highly recommended for sunset hours.

Radika Meze & Balık

Cuisine: Seafood / Meze (Turkish Tapas) Price: $$$ Signature Dish: Sea Bass in Salt (Tuzda Levrek) and warm Hot Herb starter (Ege Otları). Why it’s Top 10: If you want a traditional "Rakı-Balık" (Anise spirit and fish) night, this is the place. It offers a refined take on the classic Turkish tavern experience. Reservation: Recommended.

Serenade Restaurant & Bar

Cuisine: Mexican / Steakhouse Price: $$-$$$ Signature Dish: Fajitas and T-Bone Steak. Why it’s Top 10: A break from Turkish cuisine, Serenade is famous for its friendly service and consistently excellent international dishes. It’s a crowd-pleaser for tourists craving variety. Reservation: Good to have during high season.

Taş Konak Restaurant

Cuisine: Traditional Turkish Breakfast / Grill Price: $$ Signature Dish: "Serpme Kahvaltı" (Full Turkish Village Breakfast). Why it’s Top 10: Located by the Manavgat River, this stone mansion offers a historic atmosphere. It is widely considered the best spot for a long, lazy Sunday breakfast by the water. Reservation: Recommended for breakfast on weekends.

Q Beach Restaurant & Lounge

Cuisine: Mediterranean / Italian Price: $$$ Signature Dish: Homemade Pasta and Fresh Seafood Platter. Why it’s Top 10: Upscale beach dining at its finest. The setting is luxurious with white linens on the sand, perfect for a breezy lunch or romantic dinner. Reservation: Required for dinner.

Bistro 2000 Steak House & Sports Bar

Cuisine: European / Steakhouse Price: $$ Signature Dish: Pepper Steak and Garlic Mushrooms. Why it’s Top 10: Famous for its lively atmosphere and excellent service. It’s the go-to spot for families and groups who want a fun meal and perhaps to catch a football game. Reservation: Walk-ins welcome.

Orfoz Restaurant

Cuisine: Seafood Price: $$$ Signature Dish: Calamari and Grilled Red Mullet (Barbun). Why it’s Top 10: Situated near the Apollo Temple, Orfoz is legendary for its location and fresh daily catch. Eating fresh fish while looking at ancient ruins is a unique Manavgat experience. Reservation: Essential for outdoor seating.

Fine Dining (Upscale & Special Occasions)

When you want white tablecloths, sommelier service, and world-class presentation.

Karma Restaurant: (Mentioned above) The gold standard for upscale dining in the area.

Azumare Lounge: Located on the Side peninsula, this spot is more of a lounge but serves high-end bites and wines. It is famous for its sunset decks where you sit on cushions right above the rocks and sea.

Divan Antalya (Manavgat/Side Branch): If available, the Divan brand always offers reliable luxury Turkish and international cuisine with impeccable service standards.

Local Favorites (Where The Locals Eat)

Escape the tourist traps and eat where the residents of Manavgat go.

Dedem Kasap Cızbız: As mentioned, this is the "Meat Lover's" paradise. Authentic, loud, and delicious.

Şişçi İbo: Located in the Manavgat town center (not the beach area). Famous for "Şiş Köfte" (skewered meatballs) and "Piyaz" (bean salad with tahini). It is cheap, fast, and incredibly tasty.

Cihan Pide Kebap: The best spot for "Pide" (Turkish pizza). Try the Kuşbaşılı Pide (diced lamb) or Lahmacun. It is a wallet-friendly, authentic lunch spot.

Best For Views

Restaurants that offer a feast for your eyes as well as your stomach.

Panorama Lake Restaurant: Unbeatable views of the Green Canyon reservoir. The turquoise water against the mountains is breathtaking.

Apollonik Bar & Restaurant: Located practically inside the ancient ruins of Side, offering views of the Temple of Apollo and the sea.

Manavgat River Restaurants (e.g., Sultan Sofrası): While some can be touristy, the view of the cool, green Manavgat river rushing by while you eat fresh trout is a classic experience.

Romantic Spots

Perfect for proposals, anniversaries, or date nights.

Elia Restaurant: The dim lighting, soft jazz, and harbor view make it incredibly romantic.

Q Beach Restaurant: There is nothing more romantic than a private table on the sand under the stars with the sound of waves just meters away.

The Harbour Restaurant: Located in Side harbor, offering intimate seating and a classic sunset view over the boats.

Family Friendly

Great for kids, large groups, and picky eaters.

Bistro 2000: Very welcoming staff who are great with kids. The menu is huge, ensuring there is something for everyone (burgers, steaks, pasta, local dishes).

Taş Konak: The garden area by the river gives kids plenty of space to run around while parents finish their Turkish tea.

Side House Café & Bar: A relaxed atmosphere with a diverse menu including pizzas and burgers, plus high chairs and a friendly vibe toward families.
